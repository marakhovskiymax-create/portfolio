Context

One reliable flow from assignment to dispatch

EME.APP supports the people who receive, pick, verify, pack, and hand off orders. My work focused on turning time-sensitive warehouse tasks into a clear mobile workflow.

Warehouse staff needed to move quickly between orders while staying certain about the right item, quantity, location, and next action.

I mapped the critical warehouse paths, reduced decisions at each step, and designed feedback for scanning, quantity checks, exceptions, and handoffs.

I created a focused operational app with a prioritized queue, explicit scan states, recoverable exceptions, and clear confirmation before dispatch.

My role

I worked across EME.APP, from task prioritization and picking sequences to barcode feedback, packing, dispatch, exception states, and reusable mobile patterns. I collaborated with product and engineering to refine workflows before release.

Showing what needs attention next

I designed a prioritized queue that makes the current workload, deadlines, and next task clear at a glance.

Queue & Priority

Grouped tasks by operational urgency and kept the information required to start visible without opening each order.

Design decision

I kept the queue focused on three questions: what to pick, where to find it, and when it is needed. The same priority language continues through every task state.

Turning every scan into clear feedback

I connected barcode scanning with item, quantity, and location checks so each action confirms progress or explains how to recover.

Immediate Feedback

Designed clear success, mismatch, and retry states that can be understood quickly in a busy warehouse environment.

Keeping dispatch moving under pressure

I worked on packing, final checks, handoff, and exception recovery so completed work never becomes an ambiguous state.

Operational Confidence

Used clear progress, explicit confirmations, and recoverable errors to keep irreversible steps safe without slowing the team down.

System

The challenge wasn’t the number of taps

The app needed to minimize effort while still preventing the wrong item, quantity, or order from moving forward.

01 / Initial Assumption

Fewer confirmations?

The first instinct was to remove every step that looked repetitive.

02 / Operational Risk

Mistakes travel downstream

A wrong scan or quantity can affect packing, dispatch, and delivery.

03 / Design Decision

Confirm the irreversible moments

I kept feedback instant and reserved explicit confirmation for actions that are difficult to undo.

Impact

From warehouse tasks to a reliable handoff

Across the app, I connected the essential warehouse stages, established reusable mobile patterns, and made every completed action visible to the next person in the operation.
